Fixed camera: The scanner tracks the swimmer's position via motion centroid — where movement is happening in the frame. Works whether zoomed in or out. Optionally tap the pool walls on the video for precise calibration; otherwise it auto-calibrates from total movement vs. entered distance.
Walking with swimmer: Tracks background motion (lane lines, pool bottom, deck sliding through the frame) using 8 horizontal strips. Your walking speed = swimmer speed.
Stroke detection: Motion energy in the center of the frame reveals stroke rhythm. Breaststroke has sharp peaks with quiet glides; freestyle is more continuous.
